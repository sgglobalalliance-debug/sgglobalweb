# SG GLOBAL ALLIANCE LIMITED — WORLD-CLASS FIGMA WEBSITE PROMPT

## Role
Act as a world-class multidisciplinary digital agency consisting of:
- Brand strategist
- UX/UI designer
- Enterprise web designer
- Design-system architect
- Conversion-focused copywriter
- Accessibility specialist
- Front-end architect preparing the design for React/Next.js deployment on GitHub + Vercel

Design a premium, international-standard corporate website for **SG Global Alliance Limited**, a Nigerian consulting and solutions group with six specialist business arms:
1. SG Technology
2. SG Construction
3. SG Logistics
4. SG E-Commerce
5. SG E-Learning
6. SG Agriculture

Use the supplied SG Global Alliance logo and subsidiary logos as the starting identity. **Do not discard the existing brand equity. Modernize and standardize it.**

## Brand
Primary promise: **INSPIRE NEXT**
Brand idea: connect strategy, technology and execution to help people, businesses and institutions move to next-level performance.

The supplied identity uses:
- Royal/enterprise blue
- Charcoal / graphite
- Gold
Keep these colors. Improve their consistency, contrast and accessibility.
Recommended digital palette:
- Primary Blue: #0057A8
- Deep Blue: #003B73
- Charcoal: #29323A
- Gold: #C69A32
- Off-white: #F7F9FC
- White: #FFFFFF
Use the subsidiary logo accent colors only as controlled secondary accents.

## Logo modernization
Create a clean, professional logo system using the supplied SG monogram:
- Preserve the recognizable SG form.
- Simplify unnecessary detail.
- Keep blue + charcoal + gold.
- Create master horizontal logo, stacked logo, icon-only mark and monochrome versions.
- Use a consistent geometric grid and optical spacing.
- Ensure legibility at favicon/mobile sizes.
- Do NOT introduce unrelated symbols.
- The final look should feel credible beside international consulting, technology, engineering and professional-services brands.

## Design direction
Create a premium B2B group website: sophisticated, confident, spacious, modern and technology-enabled.
Avoid:
- Generic template appearance
- Excessive gradients
- Stock-photo clichés
- Neon colors
- Overly rounded “startup” UI
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive animation
- Fake statistics, fake client logos, fake awards or unverifiable claims

Use:
- Strong editorial typography
- Generous whitespace
- Precise grids
- Large high-quality imagery
- Subtle motion
- Data/technology-inspired visual details
- Architectural and infrastructure imagery
- Modern African business context without reducing the brand to a local-only aesthetic

## Suggested typography
Use a modern sans-serif family such as Inter, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans or IBM Plex Sans.
Use one family consistently with a disciplined type scale.
Headlines should be bold and editorial.
Body text should be highly readable.
Buttons should be concise and action-oriented.
