# SG GLOBAL ALLIANCE LIMITED — BRAND MODERNIZATION GUIDE

## Keep the brand equity
Retain the supplied:
- SG monogram concept
- Royal blue
- Charcoal/graphite
- Gold
- “SG Global Alliance Limited” name
- “INSPIRE NEXT” brand promise

## Recommended international-standard refinements
1. Redraw the SG monogram as clean vector geometry.
2. Reduce tiny details in the existing marks so they reproduce clearly at small sizes.
3. Standardize the S and G proportions across parent and subsidiary brands.
4. Keep the gold orbit/accent as a distinctive device, but make its stroke weight consistent.
5. Use one professional sans-serif wordmark for digital applications.
6. Create a parent-brand lockup and six subsidiary lockups on the same grid.
7. Create horizontal, stacked, icon-only, black, white and one-color variants.
8. Supply SVG, PDF, EPS and high-resolution PNG masters.
9. Define clear space, minimum size, misuse rules and color specifications.
10. Use the subsidiary descriptors consistently:
   - SG Technology
   - SG Construction
   - SG Logistics
   - SG E-Commerce
   - SG E-Learning
   - SG Agriculture

## Recommended parent palette
Royal Blue: #0057A8
Deep Blue: #003B73
Charcoal: #29323A
Gold: #C69A32
Off White: #F7F9FC
White: #FFFFFF

## Important
The supplied subsidiary logos should be treated as the starting point. The redesign should modernize and unify them rather than replacing their identities with unrelated symbols.
